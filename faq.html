What is Bugsay?

Bugsay is an online platform that makes it simple for people to look up, find, and check boat and ferry schedules for trips across the Visayas.

Can I buy my ticket directly on the website?

Yes. Bugsay allows users to book tickets directly through the platform, meaning you do not have to wait in line at traditional port counters.

Are student discounts available?

Yes. Students can apply their discount during checkout by verifying their university email address.
