Data privacy

Bugsay is an early prototype. This page explains, in plain language, what it stores.

What we collect

  • Bookings: the sailing and date, accommodation class, each passenger's name and passenger type (regular, student, senior citizen or PWD), each vehicle's type, plate number and optional cargo description, your email address, the payment method you chose, and the amounts.

What we don't collect

  • No payment details. Bugsay never asks for card numbers, CVVs, PINs or e-wallet logins; online payment is simulated in this prototype.
  • No copies of IDs. Discount IDs are checked by the carrier at the port.
  • No accounts, passwords, or tracking/advertising cookies. The ad spaces on the site are placeholders.

How it's used

Bookings are used to show your tickets back to you when you enter your reference, and to count how many seats and vehicle slots are left on each sailing. We don't sell or share your information.

Third-party services

Pages load icons from cdnjs, map tiles from OpenStreetMap, and photos from Pexels, Wikimedia Commons and other image hosts. Those services see your IP address when your browser downloads from them.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) you can ask what we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Contact the Bugsay team to make a request.
