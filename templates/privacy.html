Data privacy

Bugsay is an early prototype. This page explains, in plain language, what it stores.

What we collect

  • Trip plans: when you save a trip, we store the sailing, date, accommodation, number of passengers, the lead passenger's name and email, and the estimated fare.
  • Rewards waitlist: if you join, we store your email address and whether it's a university email.

What we don't collect

  • No payment details. Bugsay never asks for card numbers, e-wallet logins or CVVs.
  • No accounts or passwords.
  • No tracking or advertising cookies.

How it's used

Trip plans are used only to show your trip back to you when you enter its reference. Waitlist emails are used only to tell you when Rewards launches. We don't sell or share your information.

Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, icons from cdnjs, map tiles from OpenStreetMap and photos from Unsplash and Wikimedia Commons. Those services see your IP address when your browser downloads from them.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) you can ask what we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Contact the Bugsay team to make a request.
