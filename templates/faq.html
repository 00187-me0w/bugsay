A little help before you sail

Good questions. Smoother journeys.

From your first ferry ride to your weekly commute, a little preparation goes a long way.

What is Bugsay?

Bugsay is your one-stop Visayas sea travel companion: look up ferry schedules between Cebu, Leyte and Bohol, see how many seats and vehicle slots are left, and book passengers and vehicles in one transaction. It's an early prototype, so schedules and fares are reference values.

How do I find a ferry to VSU?

Visayas State University is in Baybay City, Leyte. On Schedules, choose Cebu City → Baybay City and your date. Roble Shipping and Lapu-Lapu Shipping sail this route overnight on alternating days. If nothing runs on your date, sailing to Ormoc City or Hilongos and continuing by road is a common alternative.

Can I book for my whole group at once?

Yes. One booking can hold up to {{ max_passengers }} passengers and {{ max_vehicles }} vehicles on the same sailing and accommodation class. Every passenger gets their own ticket with their name on it, all under one reference number.

How do I know if seats are still available?

Each sailing shows how many seats are left in every accommodation class, and how many deck slots are left for each vehicle type. The Availability page shows every sailing for a day at a glance and refreshes on its own. In this prototype each class starts with {{ demo_seats }} seats per sailing per day; real carrier capacities can be entered once available.

Can I bring my car or ship a truck?

Yes, on RoRo sailings that carry vehicles (fast craft and motorboats don't). Use Vehicle booking to see rates for motorcycles, cars, SUVs/vans, trucks and buses. You can add the driver and passengers to the same booking.

Are student discounts available?

Yes. Under Philippine law, students (RA 11314), senior citizens (RA 9994) and persons with disability (RA 10754) get {{ discount_pct }}% off passenger fares. Choose the passenger type for each person when you book, and bring a valid school, senior citizen or PWD ID. The carrier checks it at the port. The discount doesn't apply to vehicle rates or Bugsay's service fee.

Is there a booking fee?

Bugsay adds a service fee of ₱{{ fee_ticket }} per passenger ticket and ₱{{ fee_vehicle }} per vehicle. It's shown separately before you confirm.

How do I pay?

Choose GCash, Maya, card or pay at the terminal. In this prototype online payment is simulated: nothing is charged and Bugsay never asks for card numbers, PINs or e-wallet logins.

What happens if my trip is cancelled because of weather?

The Philippine Coast Guard can suspend sailings when sea conditions are unsafe. Cancelled sailings show as Cancelled and can't be booked. Carriers usually rebook or refund under their own policies. Check travel updates before leaving for the port.

What does "+1" next to an arrival time mean?

The sailing arrives the day after it departs. Many Cebu–Leyte trips leave at night and arrive in the early morning.
